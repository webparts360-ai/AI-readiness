What this assessment looks for

This is not an AI knowledge test. We are looking for behaviours associated with people who can help an organization discover, test and scale better ways of working.

25 behavioural questions
7 transformation signals
8–10 min estimated completion time
Answer according to what you would normally do, not what you think leadership wants to hear. There is no requirement to be an AI expert.

The assessment examines:

  • Healthy dissatisfaction with inefficient work
  • Business and process judgement
  • Experimentation and learning agility
  • Organizational knowledge
  • Influence without formal authority
  • Ability to multiply learning across others
  • Responsible judgement when using AI
